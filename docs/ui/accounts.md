# Accounts

The Accounts page lists all household accounts, including archived accounts when selected by the status filter. Owner is descriptive; every household member can see all accounts. Balances, reserved funds, and available funds come from the server and remain in the account currency.

Create an account with an opening balance and date. Edit active accounts; currency is locked after activity. Archive and restore preserve balances and history. Select an account to inspect its movements. A balance change is a signed delta, not a target balance: enter a positive amount to increase it or a negative amount to decrease it, then review and confirm. Amounts accept at most two decimal places and are sent as exact minor-unit strings.
